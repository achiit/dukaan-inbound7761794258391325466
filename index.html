Tea the way our family drinks it

In our house, the day never started without a pot of strong, fragrant chai. My relatives would argue over which leaf made the best cup, and I grew up tasting every one of them.

In 2025 I opened Valeora Tea in Dhule with one idea: sell the tea I would proudly serve at home. Every blend is chosen for aroma first, taste second, and freshness always.

Come by the shop on 100 Ft. Road, smell the leaf yourself, and take home what you like.
